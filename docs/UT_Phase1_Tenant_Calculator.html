This calculator is designed to be transparent about what it does and doesn't account for. Here's exactly how each figure is worked out, and where the real-world uncertainty lives.

Demand

  • Based only on what you enter — a bill kWh figure or a cost, which is converted to kWh using the grid rate.
  • We're deliberately not offering an EPC-based estimate for Phase 1. An EPC rating mostly reflects heating and fabric performance, not appliance electricity use, so it's a weak proxy here. EPC-derived heat demand becomes relevant once fabric and heat pump phases are added.

Solar

  • Generation = array size × 950 kWh/kWp/yr (fixed Scotland-average pre-loss irradiance) × 0.8 (UK industry-standard performance ratio). Not measured output, and not adjustable per-site here.
  • Real generation varies with orientation, shading, exact location and weather — Glasgow real-world post-loss yields alone range roughly 745–900 kWh/kWp/yr depending on source.
  • Self-consumed solar is capped at your actual demand: if the array would generate more than the home uses, the excess is exported, not self-consumed, and isn't counted as a saving.
  • Self-consumption above roughly 50–60% typically implies daytime occupancy or shifted appliance use alongside generation, not generation alone.

Battery

  • Assumed to cover a fixed 50% of demand remaining after solar, charged overnight at the off-peak rate. Not adjustable here, and doesn't account for battery capacity limits on individual properties.
  • Battery and inverter losses aren't modelled (typically 5–10% round-trip) — real savings will be a little lower than shown.
  • The off-peak saving assumes enrolment in a time-of-use tariff — a separate step from having the battery installed, requiring a smart meter.

Bill totals

  • Both bill totals include the same daily standing charge. It's unaffected by Phase 1, so it doesn't change the saving, but it's included so the figures reflect real bills, not unit spend only.
  • This is Phase 1 only (battery + solar). Later phases aren't reflected here.
  • Figures are illustrative estimates, not a guarantee — actual savings depend on your household's real usage pattern, weather, and tariff availability in your area.
